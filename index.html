About Wildcat Lost & Found

Wildcat Lost & Found is the official digital lost and found management system of Cebu Institute of Technology – University (CIT-U). Built to serve students, faculty, and staff, the platform makes it easy to report missing items, discover found belongings, and securely verify ownership — all from one place.

  • Secure Supabase-powered authentication
  • Role-based access for Students, Faculty, and Admins
  • Private claim evidence — only visible to administrators
  • Real-time notifications and messaging
  • Mobile-friendly and always accessible
